Red Box ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you play Red Box or visit our website.

Red Box does not require you to create an account or provide your name or email address to play the game.

However, certain information may be automatically collected by the game and third-party services used by Red Box, including:

  • Device and device-related information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address and general location information
  • Game usage and interaction information
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Advertising and analytics information
  • Information related to in-app purchases

2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

Information collected through Red Box and its third-party services may be used to:

  • Provide and operate the game
  • Improve gameplay and user experience
  • Analyze game usage and performance
  • Display and measure advertisements
  • Process and verify in-app purchases
  • Detect fraud, abuse, and security issues
  • Improve the stability and functionality of the game

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Red Box uses third-party services that may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies.

  • Google Play In-App Purchases is used to process purchases made within Red Box. Payment processing is handled by Google Play. Red Box does not directly collect or store your complete credit card, debit card, or banking information.

These third-party providers may process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms of service.

Red Box may display advertisements through Google AdMob. Depending on applicable laws, user settings, consent, and advertising configuration, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Users may have controls available through their device settings or Google's privacy and advertising tools.

Red Box offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play. Purchases are processed by Google Play and are subject to Google's terms and policies.

We do not directly receive or store complete payment card or banking information used to make purchases.
